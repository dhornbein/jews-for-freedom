# About Us

Jews for Freedom is a new collaborative effort bringing Jews together to secure our democracy. Led by partner organizations and energized by many more Jewish social justice groups, we amplify collective actions and organize new initiatives.

## Who’s involved

Jews for Freedom is an initiative of **Powered By Faith**. Our partners include:

- Bend the Arc - a partnership for justice
- Collaborative for Jewish Organizing
- the Jewish Social Justice Roundtable
- Jews United for Justice
- The Religious Action Center of Reform Judaism

Together we are responding to a call for faith communities in the U.S. to address the current crisis and forge new bonds of solidarity.
